Rules for Screen Enclosures (Lady Lake / Lake County Portion)

  • Architectural Review Committee approval is required before work starts; a separate city/county building permit is still needed.
  • Approved roof styles: mansard, dome, hip, gable-hip, gambrel.
  • Frame and screen: black, bronze, or white.
  • Must be in the rear or side quadrant, not in easements, and no taller than the existing roof peak.
  • Pool equipment concealed from the street and kept two feet from property lines.

Source: Architectural Review Manual, Lady Lake/Lake County Portion; District FAQs. Sumter and Marion portions have their own manuals (not yet summarized).
