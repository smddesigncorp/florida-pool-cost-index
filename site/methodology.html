Methodology

Not Advice

Not legal, financial, or contracting advice. Figures are labeled by source; confirm pricing with licensed contractors.

Data Labels

  • Official Permit Data County-published permit reports. Declared job values, which may be below contract prices. County-level aggregates only.
  • Third-Party Aggregate Permit totals reported by others (e.g., HBWeekly).
  • Published Estimate Ranges from online estimators; not real quotes.
  • Builder-Contributed Anonymized quotes contributed by a Florida builder: line items exactly as written in each proposal, county-level only, labeled and filterable. Currently one builder's quotes (small sample, statuses mixed: quoted, signed, lost). Their pool and screen enclosure prices include permitting costs, so compare them with quotes that also include permits. Medians shown only with 3+ quotes; otherwise the raw range. A renovation quote (no new pool) is excluded from pool totals but its enclosure figure is used. Enclosure figures that bundle a solid roof section are shown separately.
  • Pending Not yet collected.

Important Caveat on Pool Permit Values

In Lake County, most residential inground pool permits declare nominal job values (2026 Jan–Sep: 351 of 437 parsed under $5,000), so they're not used as pool prices. Screen enclosure permit values look like real prices and are shown.

Published Estimate Sources

  • EstimateFlorida (2026 Florida Prices)
  • LatestCost (Florida Pool Cost Calculator 2026) – Reports an average of $78,000 for an installed inground pool; screened enclosure 'roughly $8,000-$20,000 or more'.
  • PoolPad (Florida Typical Project) – Florida typical project $68,000; upper bound listed as $133,000+.
  • ExpertBuildCalc (Screen Enclosure Calculator) – $8-$22 per sq ft of footprint; most backyard enclosures $8,000-$35,000.
  • BuildMat Insight (Florida Pool Size & Scope) – Standard screen (110 mph) $18-$24/sq ft footprint; HVHZ coastal $28-$38/sq ft.
  • AutomatedEstimating (Screen Enclosure Calculator) – Wind-borne debris and HVHZ zones can raise the aluminum portion 15-30% versus a standard zone.
