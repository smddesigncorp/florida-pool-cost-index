Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This page explains what information the Florida Pool Cost Index collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. In short: price data on this site is anonymized, and your contact details are only shared with pool builders when you ask for quotes.

When You Ask for Quotes

The Get 3 Quotes form collects your name, phone number, email, city, county, project details (pool type, spa, screen enclosure, budget range, timeline, best time to reach you and any notes), and the date and time you checked the consent box.

  • We share your request with up to 3 licensed local pool builders who serve your county, so they can contact you about your project by phone, text or email.
  • Builders who receive a request pay this site a referral fee. That's how the site stays free.
  • We don't sell or share your details with anyone else, such as marketers or data brokers, and we never publish them.
  • We may follow up once to ask whether a builder reached you, so we can keep builder quality high.

When You Share a Quote

The Share a Quote form collects the files you upload, your county, and optionally your city, the month of the quote, whether permits were included, the quote status, and your email.

  • Uploaded files are stored privately and reviewed by hand. They're never published or shared with builders.
  • Only anonymized totals are added to the county figures, once there are enough quotes to share without identifying anyone. Names, addresses and builder names stay private.
  • Feel free to black out names, addresses and phone numbers before uploading.

Builders Talking to You

Once a builder has your request, their own privacy practices apply to how they contact you. If you'd rather not hear from a builder, tell them directly or ask us, and we'll pass that along.

Site Visits

We don't use advertising cookies or sell browsing data. We may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics that count page visits without identifying you. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security.

How Long We Keep It

Quote requests are kept as long as needed to connect you with builders and handle billing with them, then deleted on request. Uploaded quote files are kept while they're used for the county figures.

Your Choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent. Reply STOP to any text from a builder to stop texts from that builder.

Contact: To ask about your data or request removal, reply to any email from us or use the Share a Quote page.

This site is for people in Florida and isn't directed at children under 13. We may update this page; changes will show a new date at the top.
