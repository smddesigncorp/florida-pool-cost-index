🔒 Your files go privately to the site owner. They're never published. We only add anonymized totals to the county averages.

What to upload

  • The full quote or proposal (a PDF, or clear photos of every page)
  • Pool quotes, screen enclosure quotes, or both
  • Quotes you signed, are still thinking about, or turned down
  • Feel free to black out names, addresses and phone numbers first

Helpful details

If these aren't already on the quote, you can put them in the file name, or upload a photo of a quick handwritten or typed note:

  • County and community
  • Month of the quote
  • Permits included? (yes / no / unsure)
  • Status: quoted, signed or completed

What happens next

We review each quote by hand and take out any personal info. It counts toward county figures once there are enough quotes to share without identifying anyone. Your shared quotes unlock detailed breakdowns by county, pool type, enclosure style and screen type. Builder names stay private.
